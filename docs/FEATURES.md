# Features & Pedagogical Principles

## Principles

The app is built upon scientifically proven second-language acquisition principles:
- **Spaced Repetition System (SRS)**: Reinforces vocabulary right before forgetting occurs.
- **Contextual Learning**: Every single word is paired with a clear, verified example sentence.
- **Multimodal Acquisition**: Dual British and American audio pronunciation alongside phonetic IPA.
- **Active Recall**: Three dynamic quiz modes testing reading, listening, and speed.

## Feature Overview

- **Flashcard Study**: Flip, navigate, and mark words as Known / Unknown.
- **Spaced Repetition**: 5 review stages with automated scheduling.
- **Audio Quiz**: Listen to native audio with hidden text to sharpen listening comprehension.
- **Speed Race**: 30-second time attack challenge (+2s per correct answer).
- **Word Explorer**: Search by English word, translation, CEFR level, or personal note.
- **Offline Sync**: Share progress across devices via URL hashes or compact OX6 export codes.
